Vision & Mission 2026

Empowering Satu University students through confidence and continuous learning.

Our Vision

To build a Career-Ready and Sustainable Community where learning English is perceived not as a daunting academic chore, but as a joyful pathway to global opportunities and self-expression.

Our Mission

  • Inclusive & Fun: Eradicate the anxiety of speaking English via supportive peer circles and gamification.
  • Flexible Shifts: Provide adaptable study shifts that seamlessly respect university class schedules.
  • Career Preparation: Deliver workshops on English interviews, resume reviews, and public speaking.

Who is Onelish?

A student organization for building English fluency, confidence, and career readiness.

To make Onelish an inclusive, flexible, and interactive student organization built on fun learning, improving members’ English skills and confidence, and developing their careers.

Our Culture

Values that shape every meeting

Learning English can be fun, expressive, and empowering. We grow through four values:

  • Growth: every member improves at their own pace.
  • Intelligence: we think sharply and solve problems together.
  • Communication: speaking up is practiced, not feared.
  • Determination: we keep showing up, even when it is hard.

How we work

  • Weekly shift system, so class schedules never block participation.
  • Personal touch: every member is noticed and appreciated.
  • Flash evaluation of 5 to 15 minutes after each activity.
  • Monthly dues collected at the first meeting for transparent bookkeeping.
Organization

Our Structure and Divisions

Each division has a clear focus in running Onelish.

Core Board (DPI)

President, Secretary, and Treasurer. Strategic direction, official correspondence, and financial transparency.

Academic

Designs weekly activities, interactive games, quizzes, and the Bi-Monthly Skill Up content.

Business Development

Runs the paid Boardgame Station and fundraising ideas, and manages the game inventory.

Human Resources (HRD)

Member attendance, team building, weekly shift scheduling, and flash evaluations.

Project Officer

Leads major events such as seminars and internal competitions, so Academic can focus on weekly meetings.

Public Relations & Publication

External partners and English Club visits. Social media and documentation that show how fun Onelish is.

Weekly Routine

Learning every week, without the clash

Our weekly gatherings run in two shifts, and attendance counts in either one.

  • Boardgames and interactive games that turn grammar into play.
  • “Minggu VC” weeks for online internal meetings, to prevent burnout.
  • Clear rules, so every shift stays fair and organized.
